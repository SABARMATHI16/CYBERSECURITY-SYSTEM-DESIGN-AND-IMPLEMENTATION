Paper 03
Title

DRL-GAN: A Hybrid Approach for Binary and Multiclass Network Intrusion Detection

Year

2024

Publication

Sensors – MDPI

1. Problem Addressed

The paper addresses the challenge of detecting different categories of network attacks and improving intrusion classification using a hybrid learning approach.

2. Proposed Approach

The proposed DRL-GAN framework combines deep reinforcement learning and generative adversarial network techniques for binary and multiclass intrusion detection.

3. Technologies / Methods Used
Deep Reinforcement Learning
Generative Adversarial Networks
Binary Classification
Multiclass Classification
Network Intrusion Detection
4. Relevance to Our Project

The hybrid-learning concept is relevant to our proposed IDS because our system also aims to combine different detection mechanisms instead of depending on a single classifier.

5. Limitation

The paper concentrates on the learning and classification process and does not provide a complete threat-intelligence, risk-scoring and automated-response pipeline.

6. Research Gap

There is an opportunity to connect advanced intrusion classification with contextual threat information and risk-based response.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our IDS will use detection results as inputs to a risk engine, allowing the system to determine whether traffic should be monitored, alerted or blocked.
