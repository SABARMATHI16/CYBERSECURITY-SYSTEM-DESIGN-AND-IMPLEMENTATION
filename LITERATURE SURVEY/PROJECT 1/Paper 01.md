Paper 01
Title

A Deep Learning/Machine Learning Approach for Anomaly Based Network Intrusion Detection

Authors

Reem Almuhanna, Samia Dardouri

Year

2025

Publication

Frontiers in Artificial Intelligence

DOI

10.3389/frai.2025.1625891

1. Problem Addressed

The paper addresses the increasing complexity of cyberattacks and the difficulty of detecting both known and emerging attacks using conventional intrusion detection approaches.

2. Proposed Approach

The authors propose a hybrid anomaly-based Network Intrusion Detection System that combines multiple machine-learning and deep-learning techniques for network attack detection.

3. Technologies / Methods Used
XGBoost
Random Forest
Graph Neural Networks
LSTM
Autoencoders
SMOTE
Weighted soft-voting ensemble
CICIDS2017 dataset
4. Relevance to Our Project

This paper is highly relevant to our project because our proposed IDS also uses a hybrid detection approach combining machine-learning-based detection and anomaly detection.

5. Limitation

The study mainly focuses on detection and classification performance. It does not provide the complete combination of threat intelligence, unified risk scoring and automated response proposed in our system.

6. Research Gap

There is scope for extending hybrid intrusion detection by integrating detection results with threat intelligence, risk assessment and automated security responses.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our system will use the hybrid detection concept and extend it with a risk engine, threat-intelligence enrichment, automated response and cloud-based security monitoring.
