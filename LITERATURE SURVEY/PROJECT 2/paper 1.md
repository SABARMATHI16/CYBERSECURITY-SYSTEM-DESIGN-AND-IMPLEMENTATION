Paper 01
Title

Continuous Authentication in the Digital Age: An Analysis of Reinforcement Learning and Behavioral Biometrics

Authors

Priya Bansal, Abdelkader Ouda

Year

2024

Publication

Computers – MDPI

1. Problem Addressed

Traditional authentication generally verifies a user at the beginning of a session. If an attacker obtains valid credentials, the attacker may continue using the session without being detected.

2. Proposed Approach

The paper investigates continuous authentication using behavioral biometrics and reinforcement learning to identify abnormal user behavior during a session.

3. Technologies / Methods Used
Behavioral Biometrics
Keystroke Dynamics
Reinforcement Learning
Anomaly Detection
Continuous Authentication
4. Relevance to Our Project

This paper directly supports our idea of adding behavioral analysis as a security layer above conventional authentication.

5. Limitation

The study mainly focuses on keystroke behavior and continuous authentication rather than combining multiple contextual factors such as device, IP, location and access time.

6. Research Gap

A broader adaptive authentication system can combine behavioral and contextual information to calculate a dynamic authentication risk.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our system will combine behavioral and contextual signals and use the resulting risk level to determine Allow, MFA or Block.
