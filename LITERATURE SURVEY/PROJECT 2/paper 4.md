Title

Privacy-Preserving Federated Learning Framework for Risk-Based Adaptive Authentication

1. Problem Addressed

Risk-based adaptive authentication needs to analyze different types of user information while protecting sensitive data and maintaining reliable risk assessment.

2. Proposed Approach

The paper proposes FL-RBA2, a federated-learning framework for risk-based adaptive authentication that supports personalized risk modeling without centralizing raw user data.

3. Technologies / Methods Used
Risk-Based Adaptive Authentication
Federated Learning
Behavioral Features

Interaction Features

4. Relevance to Our Project

This paper is highly relevant to our risk-scoring component, particularly because it explicitly combines behavioral and contextual information for adaptive authentication.

5. Limitation

The proposed framework focuses on federated learning and privacy-preserving distributed learning, which may be more advanced than what is required for the initial prototype of our project.

6. Research Gap

A practical cloud-based implementation can simplify the architecture while retaining the central concept of behavior/context-based risk assessment.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our system will implement a practical risk engine that analyzes user context and behavior and dynamically chooses Allow, MFA or Block.
