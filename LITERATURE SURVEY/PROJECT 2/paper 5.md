1. Problem Addressed

The paper reviews continuous authentication approaches that use behavioral biometrics to continuously determine whether the current user is legitimate.

2. Research Approach

It systematically examines research involving behavioral biometric authentication, intelligent approaches and continuous user verification.

3. Technologies / Methods Covered
Behavioral Biometrics
Continuous Authentication

Contextual Information
Mobile Authentication
4. Relevance to Our Project

The paper provides recent research background for our use of behavioral analysis as an additional authentication layer.

5. Limitation

As a systematic review, it does not itself implement our complete cloud-based risk-scoring and adaptive-response architecture.

6. Research Gap

There is scope for integrating behavioral profiling with contextual risk analysis and real-time adaptive authentication decisions.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our system will combine behavioral and contextual information into a unified risk score and dynamically determine the required authentication level.
