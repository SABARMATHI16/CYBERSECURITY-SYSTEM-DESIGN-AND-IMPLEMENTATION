Paper 03
Title

SoK: A Systematic Review of Context- and Behavior-Aware Adaptive Authentication in Mobile Environments

Authors

Vyoma Harshitha Podapati, Divyansh Nigam, Sanchari Das

Year

2025

Publication

HAISA 2025 / arXiv

1. Problem Addressed

The paper investigates the growing need for authentication systems that dynamically consider user behavior and contextual information instead of relying only on static authentication.

2. Existing Research Analyzed

The study analyzes 41 peer-reviewed studies on adaptive authentication in mobile environments.

3. Technologies / Methods Covered
Context-aware authentication
Behavior-aware authentication
Machine Learning
Continuous Authentication
Anomaly Detection
Location-aware approaches
Risk-based approaches
4. Relevance to Our Project

This paper is one of the closest references to our project because our proposed system also combines behavioral and contextual information for adaptive authentication.

5. Limitation

The paper is a systematic review rather than a complete implementation of our proposed cloud-based system.

6. Research Gap

The review indicates opportunities for developing practical adaptive-authentication systems that consistently integrate behavioral, contextual and intelligent decision mechanisms.

7. Proposed Contribution in Our Project

Our project will implement a cloud-based risk engine that combines behavioral and contextual signals and produces an adaptive authentication decision.
